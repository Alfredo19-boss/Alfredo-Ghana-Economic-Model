Alfredo Ghana Economic Model: site package

Upload everything in this folder to the top level of your repository
alfre-do-ghana-economic-data, on the main branch. Keep the folder names.
The .github folder may be hidden on your computer; it must be uploaded too.

The model will then be at:
https://alfredo19-boss.github.io/alfre-do-ghana-economic-data/model/

Then, in the repository, open Actions, choose "Update economic model data"
and press "Run workflow" once to check that it works. After that it runs hourly.

Nothing in your existing files is replaced.
